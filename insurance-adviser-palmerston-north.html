Who We Work With

Typical Palmerston North clients

Manawatū is not Auckland, and the advice should not pretend otherwise. Household budgets, property prices, and the mix of self-employed and salaried work here all shape what sensible cover actually looks like.

01

Young families with a new mortgage. Usually the first serious look at insurance. The priority is making sure the mortgage does not become the surviving partner's problem, without over-insuring to the point the premium becomes unaffordable and gets cancelled in two years.

02

Self-employed and contractors. No sick leave, no employer cover, and often no ACC top-up beyond the minimum. Income protection structured correctly matters far more here than it does for salaried employees, and it is the cover most often set up wrong.

03

People whose premiums have jumped. A very common reason people call us. Sometimes the fix is restructuring to level premiums, sometimes it is adjusting the excess or sum insured, and sometimes the honest answer is that the existing policy is already good and should be kept.

04

Anyone who has never had their KiwiSaver looked at. Default funds, wrong contribution rate, or missing the annual government contribution entirely. These are small, boring fixes that compound into real money over 20 or 30 years.

Technically a licensed Financial Advice Provider.
