Local & Independent

A Palmerston North broker, not a call centre

Mutual Solutions Limited is a licensed Financial Advice Provider (FSP729871) based on Broadway Avenue in central Palmerston North. Upmeet Sodhi (FSP250405) is the adviser you deal with, start to finish, from your first conversation through to settlement.

Going direct to your own bank means you only see that bank's rates and lending criteria. A broker compares across multiple lenders, so if one bank's servicing calculator says no, or their rate isn't competitive, we can go to another lender rather than you having to start the whole process again yourself.

We are independently owned and paid the same commission by the lender regardless of which one you end up with, so the recommendation is based on what actually fits your situation, not which bank we're tied to.

Who We Help

Mortgage clients we work with

Manawatū's property market and the mix of self-employed and salaried borrowers here shape what a workable loan application actually looks like. We structure the application around your real financial picture, not a generic template.

01

First home buyers. Understanding deposit requirements, First Home Grant and First Home Loan eligibility, and how much you can actually borrow before you start looking at properties, so you're not disappointed after finding the right place.

02

Self-employed borrowers. Banks want financials presented a very specific way. We package the application correctly with your accountant's figures, which is where most self-employed applications go direct-to-bank and get declined unnecessarily.

03

People refinancing. Whether your fixed rate is expiring, you want to consolidate debt, or your circumstances have changed, we compare what your current bank offers against the rest of the market before you re-fix anything.

04

Growing families needing to restructure. Moving to a bigger property, adding a second income, or coming off parental leave all change what a bank will lend and how the loan should be structured.

Common Questions

Quick answers

Does a mortgage broker cost anything in Palmerston North?

No. We're paid a commission by the lender if your loan goes ahead, at no extra cost to you — you get the same rate you'd get going direct, plus someone comparing lenders on your behalf. See our disclosure statement →

Which banks and lenders do you work with?

We're not tied to any single bank. We work across multiple New Zealand lenders and compare rates, cash contributions, and lending criteria to find what actually fits.

Can you help self-employed borrowers get a mortgage?

Yes — this is one of our most common mortgage clients. Self-employed applications need to be packaged the way each lender wants to see them. Read the self-employed mortgage guide →

Where is your Palmerston North office?

Level 2, 26 Broadway Avenue, in central Palmerston North. We can also meet at your home, your workplace, or over video call.

Do you only help first home buyers?

No. We help first home buyers, people refinancing, and those restructuring lending as their situation changes.
